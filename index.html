How It Works

From notebook to production
in minutes, not months

Three steps. Zero infrastructure headaches.

01

Connect your repo & register your model

Point Helix at your GitHub repo or upload model artifacts directly. Helix auto-detects framework, dependencies, and hardware requirements.

02

Configure pipelines and eval gates

Define your promotion criteria — accuracy thresholds, latency budgets, and custom metrics. Helix runs evaluations automatically on every push.

03

Deploy with one click, monitor forever

Push to production with confidence. Continuous monitoring catches drift and anomalies. One-click rollback if anything goes wrong.

helix.yaml
# helix.yaml — deploy config
model:
  name: fraud-detector-v3
  framework: pytorch
  artifact: ./checkpoints/best.pt
serving:
  replicas: auto
  hardware: gpu-t4
  max_latency_ms: 150
gates:
  - accuracy >= 0.94
  - f1_score >= 0.91
# helix deploy ✓ gates passed
# → canary 10% → prod in 2m 🚀
